# Monthly Insights: this month at a glance

> Technical details: [Monthly Insights developer documentation](../features/monthly-insights.md)

## What it's for

See where your money has gone this month, which three categories cost you most, and how many days in a row you've stayed within your budget.

## Before you start

You need at least one expense. To try it out, click **Load sample data** on the **Dashboard**.

## Step by step

### Open Monthly Insights

Click **Insights** in the top bar. The page shows the current month, for example "November 2026".

- **The ring chart** shows this month's spending split by category. The total is in the middle. Each category keeps the same colour everywhere in Spendwise.
- **Top 3 categories** lists your three biggest categories this month, largest first.

If you haven't spent anything yet this month, you'll see **No spending yet this month** instead.

### Set your monthly budget

1. In the **Budget streak** box, type how much you want to spend each month in **Monthly budget (AUD)**, for example `2000`.
2. Click **Save budget**.

Spendwise splits the budget evenly across the days of the month to give you a daily allowance. With $3,000 in a 30-day month, that's $100 a day.

### Read your streak

The big number is how many days in a row, up to and including today, you've spent at or under your daily allowance. Days when you spent nothing count too.

- Go over the allowance on a day, and the streak starts again from the next day.
- If you're over today already, the streak shows 0. Tomorrow is a fresh start.
- The bar underneath shows how much of this month's budget you've used. It turns red if you go over.

### Change your budget

Click **Edit budget**, enter the new amount, then click **Save budget**. Your streak is worked out again straight away from your expenses.

## Good to know

- The streak only counts back as far as your first recorded expense.
- Your budget is saved only in this browser, like your expenses. It isn't included in exports or backups.
